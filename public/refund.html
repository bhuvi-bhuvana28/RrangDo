Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery if the product received is damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Refund requests will be reviewed and verified by our team before approval.

Once approved, the refund amount will be credited within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

Products damaged due to customer misuse, negligence, or improper handling are not eligible for refunds.

For refund assistance, please contact our support team with your order details and proof of purchase.
