About Us

RrangoDoIndia is an emerging e-commerce startup transforming the construction material supply landscape in India. We serve as a digital one-stop destination for all construction and home improvement needs — ranging from cement, TMT bars, bricks, and sand to wall putty, paints, and finishing materials.

What sets RrangoDoIndia apart is our deep understanding of the challenges faced in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, where traditional material procurement is time-consuming, lacks transparency, and often results in delays or quality compromises.

By combining modern technology, verified suppliers, real-time geo-based pricing, and strong logistics support, we offer a more structured and reliable procurement experience.

We’ve built a robust vendor ecosystem backed by strict quality checks, and our digital platform supports both B2B (builders and contractors) and B2C (individual homeowners).

Customers can easily place orders through our website or mobile platform, with future plans to introduce physical stores for stronger trust and accessibility in local markets.

Driven by innovation and execution, our goal is to empower India’s construction ecosystem through reliable supply chains, efficient delivery systems, and fair pricing — from urban metros to small towns.
